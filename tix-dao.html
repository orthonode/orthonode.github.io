Anti-Scalp Mechanisms

Smart contracts kill scalping.

Every anti-scalp rule is enforced at the protocol level. There is no platform to lobby, no support ticket to file. The contract simply won't allow it.

🔒
Resale Price Ceiling

SPL-Governance sets a hard resale cap voted on by token holders — e.g. 115% of face value. Any transfer instruction above that ceiling is rejected by the program. Baked into the NFT at mint.

ON-CHAIN ENFORCED
👤
Wallet Age Gate

Buyers must meet a minimum wallet-age threshold (e.g. 30 days) voted by the DAO. Newly created bot wallets cannot purchase. This single rule blocks 90%+ of automated scalping operations.

SYBIL RESISTANT
🎯
One Wallet, One Ticket

The program enforces a per-wallet ticket limit per event. Mass-purchasing with multiple wallets is detectable on-chain. The DAO can vote to add stake requirements that make bot farms economically unviable.

PROGRAM ENFORCED
💎
Artist Royalty on Resale

Every secondary sale triggers an automatic creator royalty transfer (voted by DAO, typically 5%). Scalping becomes less profitable. Artists recapture value from the secondary market they historically could never touch.

AUTO-ROYALTY
🔗
Identity-Bound Entry

Gate entry requires a wallet signature at the door — not just ticket ownership. Even if a ticket is transferred beyond the resale cap via off-chain deal, the original buyer's wallet ID is flagged for future governance action.

SIGNATURE VERIFY
🗳️
Community Governance Override

If market conditions change, the DAO can vote to adjust any parameter in real-time. No waiting for a platform to update its ToS. The community is the platform. Proposals execute on-chain within the voting period.

DAO CONTROLLED
