Cookie Policy

This page explains the cookies used by ORTHONODE SYSTEMS™ (orthonode.xyz). We use a small set of essential and functional cookies to operate the site and remember your preferences.

Custom ORTHONODE Cookies

on_verified
Set when you complete the Cloudflare Turnstile human verification challenge. Expires after 1 hour. Allows you to skip the gate on return visits within the TTL.
Type: Functional · TTL: 1h · Required
on_theme
Stores your theme preference (dark or light). Defaults to dark. Expires after 365 days.
Type: Preference · TTL: 365d · Optional
on_chain
Records the last protocol chain page you viewed (e.g., polkadot_hub, arbitrum_sepolia). Expires after 7 days.
Type: Analytics · TTL: 7d · Optional
on_banner
Set when you dismiss the cookie consent banner. Expires after 365 days.
Type: Consent · TTL: 365d · Optional

Third-Party Cookies

Cloudflare Turnstile
Used to verify that visitors are human without tracking. Cloudflare may set its own cookies per their Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You can clear all ORTHONODE cookies at any time via your browser settings. Clearing on_verified will require you to complete the Turnstile challenge again on your next visit.
