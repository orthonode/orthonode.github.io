Technical FAQ

How does Noir ZK verification differ from EVM? expand_more
Unlike the EVM which relies on re-execution of logic by every node, Noir allows us to generate zero-knowledge proofs off-chain and verify them on-chain with minimal gas overhead and absolute privacy.
What is the "Operational" chip indicator? expand_more
It represents a real-time heartbeat of our infrastructure node-cluster. If any critical sub-system fails, the indicator transitions to amber or red.
How do I integrate TON-SHA? expand_more
TON-SHA uses FunC and TACT. Integration requires our sovereign bridge adapter. See the documentation for specific RPC endpoints.
