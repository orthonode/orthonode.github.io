Protocol Workflow

How SHA Works

01

Silicon Extraction

The ESP32 microcontroller performs a low-level read of the internal eFuse blocks, extracting the factory-assigned MAC and unique hardware keys.

02

Stylus Burning

A dedicated Rust/Wasm Stylus contract receives the payload, cryptographically verifying the origin and permanently "burning" the ID into global state.

03

Permanent Proof

The hardware identity is now verified on-chain. Any third-party protocol can query the SHA registry to confirm device authenticity with sub-second latency.
